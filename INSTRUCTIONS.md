## Richard Phipps (2008), Julian Gollop (1985)

Chaos Groove is a remake by Richard Phipps of the classic Spectrum game Chaos: Battle of the Wizards by Julian Gollop.

## Introduction

*Chaos* is a battle between 2 to 8 wizards, any or all of which can be computer controlled. Most spells are used to summon creatures which are controlled by the casting wizard. Other spells have a variety of different effects.

## Starting the Game

From start menu screen select `PLAY`, before `START GAME` you can randomize name for each wizard, change computer or human control or `OFF` to remove from the board.

*Chaos* is played in a series of game turns. Each turn is played in three phases:

1. Spell selection
2. Casting
3. Movement and combat

## Spell selection

At the begining of each turn select one of the spells from the `Sepll panel` or `No Spell` to skip casting for this turn. Spells one use only except `Disbelieve` spell

## Casting

Spells can be casted on target, on game board, on self or with no target. Before casting spell eligible targets or area will be higlighted. 'Self' and 'no taged' spells are autocast. Creatures can be summoned as illusions if bottom half of the spell icon selected (cursor will chnage its apearance to indicate selection).

## Movement and Combat

### Movement

To move something, select piece from the game board and then select destination square to move it. Move all your summons before ending the turn. You may have eligible targets to attack after moving.

### Combat

To attack an enemy creature you must move into it.

Flying creatures may instead perform a ranged attack by placing the cursor over the enemy creature. Success depends on the attacker's Combat rating versus the defender's Defence rating, with a random factor involved.

Some creatures have ranged combat (for example, creatures carrying bows). Move the cursor to the target and press to fire. The target must be in line of sight.

## Win Condition

All wizards except one are destroyed.

## Spells

Each spell has an alignment:

- Chaos
- Neutral
- Law

As more Law or Chaos spells are cast, future spells of that alignment become more likely to succeed.

The higher the spell name appears, the higher its chance of success. Also indacated in spell description itself.

The overall state of the world is indicated by the number shown in status pannel if cursor hover over the wizard.

### Real / Illusion

Creatures may be summoned as:

- **Real**
  - May fail to cast.
  - Cannot be destroyed by **Disbelieve**.

- **Illusion**
  - Always succeeds.
  - Destroyed immediately by **Disbelieve**.
  - Do not leave a corpse

Otherwise they behave identically.

## Object Spells

### Magic Wood (Law)

Creates approximately eight magic trees around the wizard.

A wizard standing inside a Magic Wood gains a new spell after several turns.

### Shadow Wood (Chaos)

Allows manual placement of approximately eight shadow trees.

Restrictions:

- Trees cannot be adjacent.
- Line of sight is required.

Shadow Woods can be controlled to attack anything touching them.

### Magic Fire (Chaos)

Kills enemy units it covers and spreads randomly.

### Gooey Blob (Chaos)

Engulf units and spreads randomly.

Covered units resume movement when uncovered, except wizards, who die.

### Wall (Neutral)

Creates four wall sections near the wizard.

Walls block movement but can be flown over.

## Attack Spells

### Magic Bolt (Neutral)

Single attack.

### Lightning (Neutral)

More powerful single attack.

### Vengeance (Chaos ×1)

Based on game options if successfully cast on a wizard, destroys all of that wizard's creations.

One attack on an enemy creature.

### Justice (Law ×3)

Based on game options if successfully cast on a wizard, destroys all of that wizard's creations.

Provides three attacks and 

### Dark Power (Chaos ×3)

Three attacks.

### Decree (Law ×1)

One attack.

## Wizard Spells

### Magic Armour (Law)

Improves defence.

### Magic Knife (Law)

Improves melee attack, including against undead.

### Magic Sword (Law)

Further improves melee attack.

### Magic Bow (Law)

Grants ranged attacks against all creatures, including undead.

### Magic Wings (Neutral)

Allows long-range flight.

### Magic Castle (Law)

Creates a castle for the wizard to hide in.

### Dark Citadel (Chaos)

Creates a citadel for the wizard to hide in.

### Shadow Form (Neutral)

Increases protection and movement to three squares.

## Miscellaneous Spells

### Law (Law)

Makes the world more lawful.

### Chaos (Chaos)

Makes the world more chaotic.

### Raise Dead (Chaos)

Reanimates corpses as undead.

### Subversion (Neutral)

Converts an enemy creature to your side.

### Turmoil (Chaos)

Randomly relocates every object on the battlefield.

Only available from Magic Wood.

### Disbelieve (Neutral)

Destroys illusion creatures.

Always available and always succeeds.

# Tips

- If your wizard conrnered create an illusion of a strong creature to defend yourself.
- Cast Justice, Dark Power, or Vengeance on wizards (if game options allow).
- Alwas ride a mount, it will protect from Justice, Dark Power, or Vengeance spells and enemy attacks.
- Opening with **Lightning** can kill wizard before they get their first turn.
